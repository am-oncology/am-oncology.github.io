Private Hospitals & Practising Privileges

Practising Privileges Definition[cite: 1]

Practising privileges act as a licence a private hospital grants a consultant to treat patients using its facilities without being an employee[cite: 1]. You remain a self-employed independent contractor but utilize the hospital's clinical governance, policies, and CQC registration[cite: 1]. Approval must be obtained from the hospital's Medical Advisory Committee (MAC)[cite: 1]. The hospital typically charges a room fee of £20-45/hr[cite: 1].

Regulatory & Clinical Setup Checklist

Initial Onboarding Actions

  • Inform NHS: Notify your NHS department clinical lead of private work[cite: 1]. If offered an 11th PA, you must accept it to perform PP[cite: 1]. Add private practice to your job plan and declare it at your next appraisal[cite: 1].
  • Purchase Indemnity: Risk pricing is calculated from specialty risk, specific procedures, and gross turnover[cite: 1]. MDO routes include MPS, MDU, and MDDUS[cite: 1]. Commercial paths include PMP, SEMPRIS, or brokers[cite: 1].
  • Data Protection: Register as a data controller with the ICO[cite: 1]. Tier 1 micro-businesses run under <£632k turnover/10 staff costing £52/year[cite: 1]. Tier 2 small/medium sits under <£36m turnover costing £78/year[cite: 1].

Billing & Fee Platforms

  • Register for Billing: Hook up with Healthcode to electronically clear professional invoices[cite: 1]. Join Healthcode's free Private Practice Register (PPR) to universally link provider recognition to individual PMIs[cite: 1].
  • Fee Declaration: Create accounts with the Private Healthcare Information Network (PHIN) to declare consultation fees[cite: 1]. This is a statutory framework to openly display initial and follow-up consultation costs[cite: 1].

Corporate and Financial Structure

Engage a medical specialist or general accountant early to weigh structures[cite: 1]. The common rule of thumb points to a Sole Trader path if income sits below ~£30,000/year, transitioning to a Limited Company corporate vehicle above that line[cite: 1].

Sole Trader Set-Up[cite: 1]

Register as self-employed via HMRC (free setup)[cite: 1]. Viable business banking options include[cite: 1]:

  • Tide Sole Trader[cite: 1]
  • Revolut Pro[cite: 1]
  • Monzo Lite (Free tier available)[cite: 1]
  • Starling Sole Trader[cite: 1]
  • Mettle[cite: 1]

Limited Company Setup[cite: 1]

Form a registered legal company directly via Companies House[cite: 1]. Business bank infrastructure options include[cite: 1]:

  • Tide Registered Company[cite: 1]
  • Revolut Business[cite: 1]
  • Monzo Pro (£9/month configuration fee)[cite: 1]
  • Starling Business[cite: 1]
  • Mettle+[cite: 1]

Private Medical Insurance & CCSD Coding

Standard Insured Workflow[cite: 1]

1. Confirm patient secures prior pre-authorisation numbers from their PMI[cite: 1].
2. Deliver medical consultation or procedural intervention[cite: 1].
3. Route the structured invoice directly through the Healthcode hub[cite: 1].
4. Await electronic clearing, remittance processing, and bank release[cite: 1].

*Note: Self-pay pathways receive direct billing invoices[cite: 1]. Billing admin can be passed to an outsourcing bureau for roughly 3-6% of gross billed volumes[cite: 1].

The Primary UK Insurer Environment[cite: 1]

  • BUPA: Market leader (30% share)[cite: 1]. Strictly enforces rigid "fee-assured" caps; shortfalls or client top-ups are banned[cite: 1].
  • AXA Health: Second largest (25% share)[cite: 1]. Tracks strict fee limits alongside BUPA[cite: 1].
  • Aviva: Key player holding a 20% clinical market footprint[cite: 1].
  • VitalityHealth: Mid-tier system commanding a 15% practice footprint[cite: 1].
  • WPA: Agile niche player (<10% footprint) offering flexible fee frameworks[cite: 1].
