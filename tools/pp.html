The NHS Transition

In 2026, most Trusts require a formal "Declaration of Private Practice" under the Management of Conflicts of Interest Policy. If you are on a full-time contract, you usually owe the NHS 10 PAs before engaging in private work.

📧 Email Template: Clinical Director/Manager

Subject: Formal Notification of Commencement of Private Practice - Dr. [Name]

Dear [Clinical Director Name],

I am writing to formally notify the Trust of my intention to commence private medical practice starting [Date].

In accordance with my NHS contract and the 2026 GMC Good Medical Practice guidance, I confirm that:
1. Private work will be conducted outside of my agreed NHS Job Plan PAs.
2. I will ensure no conflict of interest arises regarding patient waiting lists.
3. My NHS commitment remains my primary professional priority.

I have updated my 'Declaration of Interests' on the Trust portal. Please let me know if we need to review my Job Plan to ensure continued transparency.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

The Registration Checklist

You cannot legally bill a patient until these 5 entities are satisfied.

1. GMC & Indemnity
  • GMC: Ensure "License to Practise" is active.
  • Indemnity: Must be "Occurrence-based" for London private work (MDU/MPS/Howden).
2. Data & Privacy
  • ICO: Register as a Data Controller (£40 fee).
  • DBS: Ensure you have an "Enhanced" certificate dated within 3 years.

3. Quality & Transparency
  • CQC: Mandatory if you treat patients in your own rooms.
  • PHIN: Mandatory fee & outcome disclosure.
4. The Billing Gateway
  • Healthcode: Register for an 'ePractice' account to send invoices.
  • CCSD: The database for procedure codes.

HMRC & Business Structure

In 2026, Making Tax Digital (MTD) is mandatory. You must use compatible software (Xero/FreeAgent).

Option A: Sole Trader

Pros: Zero setup cost, total privacy of accounts.

Cons: Pay 40-45% tax on everything over the threshold; personally liable for debts.

Best for: Annual profits < £40k

Option B: Ltd Company

Pros: Limited liability, 19-25% Corp Tax. Can pay spouse a salary (if they work in the biz).

Cons: Public filing at Companies House, higher accountancy fees (£2k+/year).

Best for: Annual profits > £50k
HMRC Checklist
  • Start: Register for 'Self Assessment' as soon as you earn >£1,000.
  • VAT: Medical care is exempt, but Legal Reports are not. If legal work exceeds £90k, you must register.
  • Annual: Submit accounts by Jan 31st. Set aside 35% of every private invoice for the tax man!

The 2026 Modern Practice

Don't hire a full-time secretary immediately. Use the "Hybrid Approach."

1. Virtual Secretary (VMT)

Services like Medserv or The Medical Secretary answer your calls and book slots in London clinics on a pay-per-minute or % basis.

2. AI Scribe (The Future)

Tools like Heidi Health or Freed record the consult on your phone and generate a GP letter in 30 seconds. This replaces traditional audio typing.

Automated Onboarding Flow
  1. Patient books via your website (integrated with Semble).
  2. System automatically sends GDPR consent and "Self-Pay" terms.
  3. Patient uploads insurance details via secure link.
  4. You see the patient; AI Scribe generates the letter.
  5. Healthcode sends the invoice automatically upon letter completion.
