Details are placed by reading each form's printed labels, which is reliable but not infallible; any box the tool could not place is listed against the form so it can be completed by hand. Tick boxes are suggestions only — review each one with the patient. Forms are copied weekly, unaltered, from the Royal College of Radiologists (national radiotherapy consent forms) and Cancer Research UK (SACT consent forms, developed with Guy's and St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust).
