Clinical Oncology Tools

Tools for the clinic, the planning room and the MDT

An open, browser-based set of calculators and literature tools, weighted towards neuro-oncology and lower GI cancer. Everything runs client-side; queries go straight from your browser to PubMed or ClinicalTrials.gov.
