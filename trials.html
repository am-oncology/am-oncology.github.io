Choose a condition (and any biomarkers), then search. Trials are matched against titles, eligibility criteria, and study descriptions.

This tool searches ClinicalTrials.gov only and shows trials with at least one recruiting or active site in the United Kingdom. Biomarker matching relies on free-text terms in trial records, so it can miss trials that describe eligibility differently or include them where the term appears incidentally — always confirm against the source record before acting on it clinically. Not a substitute for the trial protocol or the sponsor's eligibility screening. ISRCTN and CRUK/ECMC are not yet included.
