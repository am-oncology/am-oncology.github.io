      NeuroTrials UK — Sources

      ⚠ For informational use only. Always verify eligibility with the trial team before referring patients.

      Results are split into three automatically queried tiers, followed by a checklist of five sources that have no public API and must be checked manually. The checklist is shown after every search with your search term already in the links.

      ClinicalTrials.gov — UK confirmed

      Trials with one or more UK sites formally registered in the CTG record.

      ClinicalTrials.gov — Possibly recruiting in UK

      Trials recruiting internationally with no UK sites registered yet. These are worth contacting — many are available via the NIHR CRN before their UK sites are formally entered. This is how EF-41/KEYNOTE D58 and LUMEN-1 are now surfaced.

      ISRCTN

      UK-only registry. Catches trials not on ClinicalTrials.gov at all, e.g. CARGO.

      Manual checks (no public API — cannot be queried automatically)

      NIHR Be Part of Research

      NIHR's CRN portal. Often ahead of CTG for UK site registration.

      EU Clinical Trials Register (EUCTR)

      Essential for EORTC-led European trials, especially meningioma.

      UCLH Find a Study

      UCLH-specific trials opening or running at UCLH.

      Cancer Research UK Trials

      CRUK-funded and supported trials with plain English summaries.

      WHO ICTRP

      Aggregates all 17 global registries.
