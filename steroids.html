This is a planning aid and carries no prescribing authority. Equivalence tables describe anti-inflammatory potency only and do not capture differences in mineralocorticoid activity, half-life or tissue penetration — conversions between agents are approximate and should not be used to switch drugs without pharmacy input. Taper rates here are a generic default, not a protocol; the right rate depends on indication, duration, the reason for tapering and how the patient responds.
