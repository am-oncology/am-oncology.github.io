Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how AM Oncology ("we", "us") handles data when you visit our website. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

2. Data Collected by Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit this site, GitHub may collect "Log Data" which includes your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This is necessary for security and to maintain the service. We do not have access to this raw data.

For more information, please see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies

This website does not currently use any tracking or marketing cookies. If we add analytics (like Google Analytics) in the future, we will update this policy and provide a consent banner.

4. Contacting Us

If you choose to contact us via the details provided on our contact page, we will use your information only to respond to your enquiry. We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. For any privacy-related enquiries, please contact us via the details on our Contact page.
